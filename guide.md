# Palengke Helper+ app guide

Palengke Helper+ helps Filipino households plan meals, track household income and expenses, compare reference prices, and prepare grocery lists.

## Getting started

Open [Palengke Helper+](https://palengkehelper.pages.dev/) and sign in or choose Continue as visitor. Guest records are stored in the browser; clearing browser data can remove them. Use the navigation tabs to switch sections. Each section has a How to use button with English and Tagalog instructions. A theme button switches between light and dark mode.

## Features

- Home: Daily meal inspiration, a Bible verse, and shopping information.
- Budget: Monthly folders for income and expense records, with monthly, half-month, and weekly filters.
- Meal Planner: Generate or customize a weekly plan. Set the number of people (pax) to scale ingredients and costs. Open recipe details to see available reference prices and missing prices.
- Prices: Browse published DA market prices and DTI suggested retail prices, including report dates, regions, and selling units.
- Palengke AI: Ask about Filipino cooking, meal planning, grocery shopping, budgeting, and app features. AI suggestions can contain mistakes; verify ingredients and estimates.
- Grocery: Track quantities, purchased items, and available reference costs. User-entered local prices are estimates and do not replace official reference records.

## Interpreting prices

DA report prices and DTI suggested retail prices are not live store quotations. DTI prices often describe a specific brand and package size. Compare equivalent units and preserve source dates and regional qualifications. The public [price snapshot](https://palengkehelper.pages.dev/data/prices.json) may differ from newer records loaded in the app.

Missing ingredient prices produce partial subtotals, not complete meal costs. A consumed portion can cost less than buying a whole package. Planning allowances are estimates, and actual shopping costs may also include cooking fuel and additional ingredients.

## Agent access

The app is operated through its browser interface. No public MCP, A2A, or WebMCP tool endpoint is advertised. Discovery documents do not authorize account access, purchases, changes to budgets, or publishing prices. Admin publishing requires authorized access.
