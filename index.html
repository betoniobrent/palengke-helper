Tipid Tips (How to Save This Week)

• Palengke Over Supermarkets: Buy fresh meats, garlic, and onions at your local wet market instead of grocery stores to instantly shave 15-20% off shelf pricing.

• Sunset Shopping: Hit the wet market around 5 PM to 6 PM. Fish and vegetable vendors often drop their prices just to clear their remaining inventory before closing.

• The "Tingi" Trap: While sachet or single-use packing feels cheaper upfront, buying essentials like cooking oil, soy sauce, and dish soap in larger pouches saves significant money long-term.

• Repurpose Leftovers: Leftover fried fish from dinner can easily turn into delicious *Cardillo* or *Sarciado* for tomorrow's lunch with just a couple of eggs and tomatoes.
